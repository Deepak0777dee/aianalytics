About Stackly

AI-Powered Analytics, Built for Growth

We're on a mission to make analytics insightful, not invasive. Stackly empowers businesses to understand their audience without compromising privacy.

Our Story

Born from frustration with bloated analytics

Stackly was founded in 2024 by a team of developers and data scientists who were frustrated by the complexity, privacy concerns, and performance impact of traditional analytics tools. We set out to create something different — a tool that's simple enough for anyone to use, fast enough to never slow down your site, and privacy-respecting enough to never need a cookie banner.

Today, Stackly powers analytics for over 100,000 websites worldwide, from indie developers to Fortune 500 companies. We're proudly bootstrapped and focused on building the best product for our customers.

Our Values

What Drives Us

Every decision we make is guided by these core principles. We build software that respects users and delivers exceptional performance.

Privacy First

We never collect personal data, use cookies, or track individuals across sites. Your users' privacy is non-negotiable. Analytics shouldn't cost you your users' anonymity.

Performance Obsessed

Our tracking script is less than 1KB. We ensure analytics never impacts your Core Web Vitals.

Open & Transparent

We publish our data practices and firmly believe in building in the open.

Customer Obsessed

Every feature starts with customer feedback. We're building the exact tools you ask for to help you succeed.
